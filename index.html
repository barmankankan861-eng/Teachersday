01 · The invitation

Dear Teachers,
this one is for you.

With immense respect and gratitude, the students of Class XII invite all our teachers to join us as we celebrate Teachers' Day together at Physics Wallah.

Dear Teachers,

Somewhere between derivations and doubt sessions, between "concept clear hai na?" and one more numerical before the bell rang, you turned a subject we were scared of into one we now argue about for fun.

The journey is long, and some days it wears us down more than we let on. You've been the ones pulling us back — sometimes with a shortcut formula, sometimes with a five-minute story that had nothing to do with physics at all.

This Teachers' Day, we don't just want to wish you from a distance. We want you in the room with us — no boards, no bells, just an evening to say thank you properly.

With more gratitude than we know how to write an equation for,Your Class 12 Batch
